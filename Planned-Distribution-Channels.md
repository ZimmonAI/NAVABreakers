# NAVABreakers — Planned Distribution Channels

**Season 1 premieres November 30, 2026, with one full anime episode released each month and new short-form stories and highlights shared weekly between premieres.**

NAVABreakers will meet its audience directly online. Full episodes form the heart of the series; short clips and everyday adventures keep the world alive between monthly releases. Viewers can follow the series through social platforms rather than traditional anime broadcasters or major film-streaming services.

## Where to Watch and Follow

| Platform | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **YouTube** | The main home for complete monthly anime episodes, an ordered Season 1 playlist, trailers and YouTube Shorts. |
| **TikTok** | Short episode highlights and original character-centered slice-of-life stories. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Short stories, episode moments, character introductions, artwork and release announcements. |
| **Facebook Reels** | Episode highlights, character shorts, community posts and release announcements. |
| **Discord and Reddit** | Conversations about characters, episodes, mysteries and the NAVABreakers world. |
| **Webtoon channels** | Companion comic storytelling and links between comic and anime audiences. |

## Weekly Short-Form Releases

A new short-form video will appear **every week between full-episode premieres** across YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Each will run **30–59 seconds** and use one of two formats.

### Episode Cutdowns

Selected moments from the latest released episode: funny exchanges, memorable reactions, mysterious discoveries, tense confrontations and action highlights. Each clip offers an introduction to the anime and a reason to watch the complete episode on YouTube.

### Original Slice-of-Life Mini-Stories

Standalone **30–59-second stories** following James, Joir and the people around them. These are fresh, character-centered adventures—not simply trailers or recycled footage. They explore village routines, strange inventions, amusing misunderstandings, friendships and quiet moments that make this world feel like home.

## A Typical Monthly Cycle

| Week | Featured release |
| --- | --- |
| **Premiere week** | Full anime episode on YouTube; release announcement across social channels. |
| **Following week** | A 30–59-second cutdown from the newly released episode. |
| **Next week** | A new 30–59-second original slice-of-life mini-story. |
| **Final stretch before the next premiere** | Another episode highlight or slice-of-life mini-story, along with a preview of the next monthly episode. |

Short-form stories continue every week, including months when the interval between episode premieres spans parts of five calendar weeks.

## Follow the Story

Watch the main adventure unfold each month on YouTube, discover more of the characters' everyday lives each week on social media, and join the conversation as NAVABreakers grows. Every full episode moves James and Joir's story forward; every short adds another moment to their world.

**First episode: November 30, 2026. New full episodes monthly. New 30–59-second shorts weekly.**
